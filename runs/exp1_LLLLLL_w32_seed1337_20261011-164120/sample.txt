Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sunshine. One day, while playing, she found a big rock. She picked it up and started to rock. 

Suddenly, she heard a loud noise coming from outside. She went out to look for her rock. She looked around in the backyard, but the rock was a bit scary. 

Lily was frightened and started to tremble. She did not know what to say. Suddenly, the rock started to move and he fell to the ground. 

Lily knew she had to help the rock. She carefully put the rock in her pocket and made sure it was safe. The rock was strong, and Lily was happy to help. She climbed down the rock and hugged her rock friends. From then on, Lily learned that it's okay to tell someone that we can fix things, even if we don't want to break them.
