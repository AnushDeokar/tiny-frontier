Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the dirt. One day, a big storm came and took Tim's toy boat away.

Tim was sad. His mom saw that he was scared and wanted to help. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can find something fun to do." Tim was very happy and felt the water in his face.

Tim played with his toy boat all day. He made a big boat with sand and a big boat. He was very happy with his new toy. After a while, Tim's mom came and saw the big boat. She said, "Thank you for helping me out!" Tim smiled and said, "I love my toy boat! Mommy, it's my favorite toy!"
