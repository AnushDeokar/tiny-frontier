Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play games with his friends. One day, a big red ball got stuck in a big hole in the ground. Tim was sad.

Tim's mom saw him struggling and asked, "Why are you struggling, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to play in the hole." His mom smiled and said, "Let's try to find a way to get your ball."

Together, Tim and his friends played to the other side of the hole. They looked all around, but they could not find the ball. In the hole, they could not find it. Tim knew that he would get his ball back to the hole and have fun with his friends.
