Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very curious. One day, he went outside to look for some ice cream. He saw a big red lake in the distance. Tim felt a little scared, but he held his dad's hand.

Tim picked up the ice cream and looked at his dad in the cold water. He was very happy. When he got home, his dad helped him take off his helmet. He felt proud that he had a lot of fun playing with his new toy.
