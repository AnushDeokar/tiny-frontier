Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in his backyard. One day, he found a big tree. The tree was covered in dirt. Tim was so happy.

Tim picked up the tree and said, "Can I play with you?" The tree said, "Yes, here I can. But be careful in it. Don't fall." Tim listened to his mom and said, "Look, I can play with the tree. Let's go and play a game!"

As they played together, Tim went to play with his friends. They played with the tree all day and had many fun. Tim had a great time at the top of the tree. He had a good time in the tree.
