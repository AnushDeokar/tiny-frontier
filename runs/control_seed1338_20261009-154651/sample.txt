Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he found a big tree. The tree was very tall and had many branches that he wanted to touch. Tim thought the tree looked fun and he wanted to touch it.

Tim went to the tree and tried to reach the tree. He pushed and tried, but the tree was too high. Tim felt guilty for trying to climb the tree. He put his red car on the branch and went to find a safe place to stay.

Tim looked all around, but he could not find the swing. He needed help to carry it. After a while, Tim found a high branch. Tim climbed up the branch and was so happy. He thanked the tree and went back home. From that day on, Tim always remembered to be nice to others when they were there.
