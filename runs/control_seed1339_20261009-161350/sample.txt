Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he found a big tree. The tree had many branches.

Tim wanted to play hide-and-seek, but it was too high. He could not reach the tree. Tim looked for his mom in the tree. He looked in the tree, but the trees were too high.

Tim saw a big tree with many trees. He thought they looked fun. He wanted to try the tree. He climbed up the tree and went up to the tree. He sat down and listened to the birds. The birds were happy. They all sat down and played together. Tim knew that the tree was not so bad at all.
