Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the snow. One day, he found a big tree with a hole in it. He wanted to climb it and play with it. But his friend, Sam, came and saw the hole.

Tim said, "I want to find something fun. Can we ask the hole for a stick or a rock?"

Tim said, "No, we can't find that!"

Sam said, "Let's go first to the hole before we use it!"

So, they held on tight and went back to the hole. Tim was happy to have found his way back home.
